The sound follows changes in amplitude and frequency.

Amplitude

1.00
Controls the maximum displacement from equilibrium. For sound, this changes the loudness.

Frequency

440 Hz
Controls how many complete cycles occur every second. For sound, this changes the pitch.

Phase

0°
Moves the waveform horizontally relative to the zero-phase reference.
